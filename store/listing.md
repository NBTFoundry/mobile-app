## Main store listing

**App name** (30): NBT Manager

**Short description** (80):
Install and update apps on BMW NBT Evo head units from your phone.

**Full description:**

NBT Manager is the phone side of NBTFoundry's application platform for BMW NBT Evo (iDrive 6) head units.

• Pair once, with a code shown on the car's screen.
• Get apps: download signed applications (games, emulators, live gauges, Android Auto) from the NBTFoundry catalog, or add a package file yourself.
• Install, update and remove them over the car's Wi-Fi. The car checks every package's signature and the driver confirms each install on iDrive. A bad update rolls back by itself.
• Change an application's settings while it runs, send it files such as games you own, read logs and crash reports.
• First install: put NBT Manager on a car over a Wi-Fi ENET adapter.
• English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Chinese and Japanese.

Requires a car with a BMW NBT Evo head unit. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Warning: installing or modifying head-unit software can brick your head unit. Recovery may require professional service or replacement, at your expense. Provided as is; use at your own risk. Use applications only while parked.
